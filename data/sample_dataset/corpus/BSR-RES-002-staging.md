## Primordium versus mature basidiocarp

A primordium is an early stage small white to cream coloured bump at the
palm base. A mature basidiocarp is a developed bracket or conk with a
darker upper surface. Correct staging matters because management actions
differ by stage.

## Stage based actions

For primordia, the recommended actions are sanitation of the palm base,
mounding of soil to cover exposed roots, and continued monitoring. For
mature basidiocarps, the recommended actions are immediate sanitation,
isolation trenching to limit root contact spread, or controlled de-boling
of severely affected palms.

## Confidence and confirmation

When detection confidence is below 0.60, the finding should be flagged
for secondary agronomist confirmation before physical treatments are
applied. Absence of visible fruiting bodies does not mean a healthy palm
because internal trunk decay can remain active without external signs.
