# BSR-EXT-001: Basal Stem Rot Field Sanitation for Oil Palm

- **Title:** Basal Stem Rot Field Sanitation for Oil Palm
- **Author:** Estate Extension Service
- **Year:** 2023
- **Source:** Sample extension bulletin (synthetic sample for smoke testing)

## Sanitation around infected palms

When a mature basidiocarp (developed conk) is observed at the palm base,
field teams should sanitize the surrounding area promptly. Remove the
visible fruiting body, clear loose debris within a one metre radius, and
avoid wounding neighbouring healthy palms with cutting tools.

## Isolation of severe cases

Palms with repeated mature basidiocarp emergence across inspections are
candidates for isolation trenching or controlled removal (de-boling).
The decision must be confirmed by an agronomist before any physical
treatment is applied.

## Monitoring after sanitation

Revisit sanitized palms within 30 days. If new primordia (early stage
bumps) appear, record the stage transition and escalate to the plantation
agronomist for review.
